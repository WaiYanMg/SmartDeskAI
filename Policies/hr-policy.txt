Section 1.1 - Annual Leave Entitlement
Employees are entitled to 20 days annual leave per year.
Leave requests of 1-5 days can be approved automatically.
Employees must have sufficient leave balance remaining.

Section 1.2 - Leave Request Process
Requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
Maximum 5 consecutive days without manager approval.
Employee must ensure work coverage before requesting leave.

Section 1.3 - Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days paid sick leave per year.
Doctor certificate required for sick leave over 3 days.

Section 1.4 - Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
Remote work must be approved by line manager in advance.

Section 1.5 - Training Leave
Employees entitled to 3 days paid training leave per year.
Training must be relevant to current role and pre-approved.

Section 2.1 - Auto Approval Criteria
Requests of 1-3 days with adequate notice: AUTO APPROVE
Requests of 4-5 days with coverage confirmed: AUTO APPROVE
Requests over 5 days: ESCALATE for human review
Requests without coverage: ESCALATE for human review
